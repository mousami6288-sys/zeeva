Frequently Asked Questions

Everything You Need to Know

What is Ziva Catalyst?

Ziva Catalyst is the service brand of Mausmi Valambhiya, an MBA in Marketing with over 10 years of experience specializing in social media marketing and content creation across all major platforms including Google, Meta, Facebook, WhatsApp, LinkedIn, and YouTube.

What social media platforms does Ziva Catalyst manage?

Ziva Catalyst provides expert marketing and content creation services across all major platforms: Facebook, Instagram, LinkedIn, YouTube, WhatsApp Business, Google (Search & Ads), X (Twitter), Pinterest, and emerging platforms. Each platform strategy is customized based on your specific business goals and target audience.

What services does Ziva Catalyst offer?

Ziva Catalyst offers comprehensive digital marketing services including social media strategy, content creation (graphics, videos, reels, carousels), paid advertising (Google Ads & Meta Ads), brand development, community management, analytics & reporting, influencer marketing, and WhatsApp business marketing.

How much do your services cost?

Every business is unique, so pricing is customized based on your specific needs, platforms, and goals. I offer flexible packages ranging from single-platform management to comprehensive multi-platform strategies. Contact me for a free consultation to discuss your requirements and receive a tailored proposal.

How quickly can I see results from social media marketing?

While paid advertising can generate immediate results, organic social media growth typically shows significant improvements within 2-3 months. Building a strong, engaged community usually takes 3-6 months of consistent strategy execution. I provide monthly reports so you can track progress from day one.

Do you work with small businesses or only large brands?

I work with businesses of all sizes — from startups and solopreneurs to established companies. My strategies are scaled and customized to match your budget and growth objectives. Every business deserves expert social media marketing, regardless of size.

What makes Ziva Catalyst different from other digital marketing agencies?

Ziva Catalyst offers the personal attention of a dedicated expert with the strategic depth of an agency. With my MBA in Marketing and 10+ years of hands-on experience, you get direct access to senior-level expertise without agency overhead. Every strategy is personally crafted and executed — no junior handoffs.
